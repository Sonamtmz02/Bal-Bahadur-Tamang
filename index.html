Bal Bahadur Tamang, professionally known as Sonam Tamang, is a growing creative individual from Nepal.

Bal Bahadur Tamang, professionally known as Sonam Tamang, is a growing creative individual from Nepal with practical experience in photography, photo editing, studio work and content creation.
