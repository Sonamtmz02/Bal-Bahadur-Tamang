01 — About Me

Who I Am

I'm Bal Bahadur Tamang, professionally known as Sonam Tamang — a growing creative individual from Nepal with practical experience in photography, photo editing, studio work and content creation.

I currently work at Laligurans Photo Studio, where I'm involved in photography, photo making, editing, printing, photocopy and other day-to-day studio services.

I don't consider myself perfect or highly advanced in every field. I enjoy learning, practicing and improving step by step. Most of my experience has come through real-world work, experimentation and continuous learning.

Alongside my work, I'm currently studying Bachelor of Education at Chautara Multiple Campus.
